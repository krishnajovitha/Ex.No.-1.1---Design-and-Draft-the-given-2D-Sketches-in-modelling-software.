# Ex.No.-1.1-Design and Draft the given 2D Sketches in modelling software.

## AIM

To sketch the given drawing with dimensions using fusion 360 tool.

## REQUIREMENTS

  1. Autodesk fusion 360
  2. Windows 10
  3. 16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 2 GB or more)
  4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload 
  
## PROCEDURE

1.	Open new file and then select sketch icon.
2.	Select the plane for sketching. 
3.  Draw a circle of radius 20 and radius 33 in the center point of axis , using the circle tool in the create.

<img width="373" height="488" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-10-12 at 11 28 26 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/053f3f1c-3f8b-421c-b1d2-19c69a955be4" />

4.  Draw a small circle of radius 6 and align it in the circle of radius 20 . Select the circular pattern option in the create tool bar and select the circle of radius 6 and select the center point and create a circle of quantity 6 .

<img width="360" height="215" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-10-12 at 11 28 26 PM (2)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d248f169-71af-4558-a3c2-e50b4d092fcd" />

5.  Trim the unwanted part using the trim option in the modify toll bar.

<img width="267" height="346" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-10-12 at 11 28 26 PM (1)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/46eccfd1-f6f5-4d1c-bd61-b9000bdb1ff8" />

6.  Draw a circle of radius 10 and radius 16 in the top of the center circle with the vertical dimension of 65 from the center point.
7.  Draw two circle of radius 7 and radius 10 on the left side of the center circle with vertical dimension of 26 from the center point and horizontal dimension of 47 from the center point.
8.  Draw two circle of radius 5 and radius 10 below the center circle with the dimension of 50 from the center point and 24 from the center of circle with radius 7 and radius 10, using the dimension tool in the create tool bar.

<img width="373" height="488" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-10-12 at 11 28 26 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b8469ac0-055b-4955-8865-4ca48c9b53b1" />

9.  Draw a circle with radius of 20 and tangent it with the the center circle of radius 33 . Draw another circle with radius of 64 and tangent it with the circle of radius 32 in the top and the center circle of radius 33.
10. Draw a circle of radius 20 and radius 10. Tangent the circle of radius 10 with the circle of radius 10 and the center circle of radius 33 in the bottom side and the tangent the circle of radius 20 with the circle of radius 10 and the center circle with radius 33 in the bottom side, using the tangent tool in the constraint tool bar.

<img width="237" height="453" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-10-12 at 11 28 26 PM (3)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e7bf975f-f8f0-4130-a479-d68a3718f1b8" />

11. Trim the unwanted parts and mirror the left side design using the mirror option.

<img width="267" height="346" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-10-12 at 11 28 26 PM (1)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4d5ea88d-28ea-4e5d-8788-d2ca2fff2edf" />

12. Select the parts to be mirrored and select the plane to be mirrored and mirror it using the mirror tool in the create tool bar.
